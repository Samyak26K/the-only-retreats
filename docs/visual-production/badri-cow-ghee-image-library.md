# The Only Retreats — Visual Production Plan

## Product

**Himalayan Badri Cow Ghee**

## Creative Direction

This is not product photography for retail conversion.

This is origin documentation.

Every frame must feel like it could hang in a quiet gallery, appear in an Architectural Digest feature on Himalayan craft, or open a National Geographic short film about living heritage.

### Brand Feeling

Calm. Elevated. Documentary. Intimate. Timeless.

### References

Aman Resorts · Apple · Leica · Aesop · Loro Piana · Hermès · National Geographic · Architectural Digest

### Forbidden

Generic ecommerce white seamless. Overlit beauty dishes. Fake “lifestyle influencer” kitchens. Neon accents. Crowded product piles. Aggressive CTAs in frame. Plastic packaging aesthetics. Stock-photo smiles. Hyper-saturated food styling.

### Shared Prompt Prefix (append to every image)

```text
Photographed for The Only Retreats, an origin-first Himalayan heritage brand.
Cinematic museum-quality still photography.
Natural materials only. Quiet luxury. Documentary realism.
No text, no logos, no watermarks, no packaging barcodes.
Shot on medium format / Leica aesthetic, shallow to moderate depth of field,
true-to-life Himalayan color: warm sand, forest moss, muted gold, charcoal,
morning mist. Soft film grain. Editorial restraint.
```

### Technical Delivery

- Aspect ratios noted per asset
- Master files: 4K+ stills, TIFF or high-quality JPEG
- Color profile: soft, warm, slightly desaturated Himalayan palette
- Keep skin, landscape, and ghee tones honest — never plastic-smooth

4. Keep Badri cow depictions **breed-honest** — small indigenous hill cattle character, never Holstein dairy industrial look.
5. Ghee color: warm gold / butter — never neon yellow.
