# THE ONLY RETREATS

## Creative Production Plan

### Himalayan Badri Cow Bilona Ghee

**Origin:** Pulag, Rumsu & Hallan · Kullu Valley · Himachal Pradesh · ~2,460 m

**Document type:** Visual Identity & Production System  
**Status:** Awaiting approval before image generation  
**Audience:** Creative Director, Photography, Design, Content

---

# Brand Philosophy (Governing Truth)

The Only Retreats is not an ecommerce brand.

It is an origin-first luxury house documenting India’s landscapes, biodiversity, people, traditions, craftsmanship and living heritage.

Every product is not a SKU.  
Every product is a place that still exists — barely — and a way of making that may not survive another generation unless it is witnessed with dignity.

### What we sell

We do not sell ghee.

We sell the continuation of a relationship between:

- Badri cows and high pastures
- Families in Pulag, Rumsu and Hallan
- Bilona churning as living knowledge
- Clarified gold that carries altitude, patience and care into a home

### What the page must never feel like

- A supermarket product page
- A “superfood” wellness brand
- Influencer kitchen content
- Aggressive D2C conversion design
- Plastic luxury / gold foil excess
- Documentary pity or poverty tourism

### What the page must feel like

A quiet museum gallery that opens into a valley.  
A Kinfolk essay that knows the names of villages.  
An Aman arrival sequence: silence first, then meaning.  
A National Geographic short film slowed into stills.  
Hermès restraint applied to Himalayan material culture.

# 1. Website Storyboard

The product page is not a list of sections.  
It is a **film in chapters**.

Each chapter has one job and one emotion.  
If a chapter tries to sell, it fails.

---

## Chapter 01 — Hero

**Working title:** _The Jar That Carries a Valley_

**Purpose**  
Establish origin before commerce. The visitor should know, within two seconds, that they have not landed on a shop.

**Emotion**  
Quiet awe. Arrival. Soft gravity.

**Visual job**  
One dominant frame: landscape and/or jar in landscape. Typography secondary. No price in the emotional field of the first viewport if avoidable; commerce waits below the story beat.

**Visitor should feel**  
“This comes from somewhere real.”

---

## Chapter 02 — The Forgotten Path

**Working title:** _Roads That Thin Into Footpaths_

**Purpose**  
Introduce access as meaning. Pulag, Rumsu and Hallan are not convenient. The difficulty is part of the truth.

**Emotion**  
Curiosity. Slight solitude. Respect for distance.

**Visual job**  
Paths, bends, stone steps, misted ridges, vehicles left behind, human scale against mountain road. No product yet — or product only as a distant memory of where this journey ends.

**Visitor should feel**  
“Few people reach here. That is why this exists.”

---

## Chapter 03 — The Living Mountains

**Working title:** _Kullu at 2,460 Metres_

**Purpose**  
Locate the product in geography and ecology: altitude, air, pasture, seasons, biodiversity of the valley system.

**Emotion**  
Belonging to place. Oxygen. Clarity.

**Visual job**  
Wide living landscapes of Kullu’s high villages — not generic “Himalayas stock.” Specificity over postcard. Grass, stone, cedar light, monsoon cloud, winter thinness.

**Visitor should feel**  
“The land is the first ingredient.”

---

## Chapter 04 — The Badri Cow

**Working title:** _The Small Cow of the Hills_

**Purpose**  
Centre biodiversity. The Badri cow is not a dairy factory animal. She is indigenous, adapted, modest in yield, immense in meaning.

**Emotion**  
Tenderness without sentimentality. Reverence for a living being.

**Visual job**  
Portraits, herd-in-landscape, coat texture, grazing, movement on paths. Never industrial stalls. Never Holstein substitution. Breed honesty is non-negotiable.

**Visitor should feel**  
“This animal is the origin of the gold.”

---

## Chapter 05 — The Bilona Method

**Working title:** _Hand, Wood, Time_

**Purpose**  
Explain craft without instructing like a recipe blog. Bilona is cultural technology — churning, curd, butter, slow clarification — patience made physical.

**Emotion**  
Concentration. Warmth. Continuity.

**Visual job**  
Hands, bilona, vessels, fire or controlled heat, ladle, resting jars. Process as ritual. Faces optional and always dignified; hands often enough.

**Visitor should feel**  
“Nothing here was rushed.”

---

## Chapter 06 — The People

**Working title:** _Keepers of the Method_

**Purpose**  
Honour the families and communities of Pulag, Rumsu and Hallan as custodians — not as props for brand authenticity.

**Emotion**  
Dignity. Quiet pride. Human warmth held in restraint.

**Visual job**  
Work, gesture, courtyard, shared labour, silhouette, hands. Prefer consent-forward, respectful portraiture. Avoid poverty framing. Avoid staged “smiling villager” tourism.

**Visitor should feel**  
“I am meeting people, not a supply chain.”

---

## Chapter 07 — Product Passport

**Working title:** _A Record of Origin_

**Purpose**  
Give factual gravity: villages, altitude, method, animal, season, batch intimacy. Traceability as poetry, not bureaucracy.

**Emotion**  
Trust. Precision. Calm authority.

**Visual job**  
Minimal still-lives, map textures, altitude markers, batch objects, jar beside document-like paper. Editorial rows of truth in the UI; images support, never decorate.

**Visitor should feel**  
“I could find this place on a map — and that matters.”

---

## Chapter 08 — Daily Ritual

**Working title:** _How It Enters a Home_

**Purpose**  
Show use as ritual — warm, finish, nourish — never as “hack” or recipe spam.

**Emotion**  
Domestic sanctuary. Soft pleasure. Continuity from mountain to table.

**Visual job**  
Spoon, melt, simple plate, morning light, hands offering. No influencer kitchens. No crowded props.

**Visitor should feel**  
“This belongs in a quiet morning.”

---

## Chapter 09 — Packaging

**Working title:** _The Object_

**Purpose**  
Present the jar as a crafted object worthy of Hermès-level still life — form, glass, seal, gold within.

**Emotion**  
Desire without greed. Collectibility of a daily artifact.

**Visual job**  
Front, three-quarter, open lid, macro seal, size variants. Linen, stone, paper. Museum lighting.

**Visitor should feel**  
“I want to keep this, not just consume it.”

---

## Chapter 10 — Founder Note

**Working title:** _Why We Began_

**Purpose**  
Human authorship of the brand promise. Why The Only Retreats exists — witnessing, protecting, sharing with restraint.

**Emotion**  
Honesty. Soft conviction. Intimate voice.

**Visual job**  
One restrained portrait or hands-with-landscape; or a single landscape that stands in for the founder’s gaze. Typography carries the voice; image does not compete.

**Visitor should feel**  
“A person is accountable for this.”

---

## Chapter 11 — Related Products

**Working title:** _Other Origins Waiting_

**Purpose**  
Invite discovery of the wider archive of origins — never a cart-upsell carousel energy.

**Emotion**  
Curiosity. Continuity of the house.

**Visual job**  
Same photographic language for companion products. Shared stone/linen grammar. “View” not “Buy now” energy.

**Visitor should feel**  
“There is a whole map beyond this jar.”

# 3. Visual Narrative

## The Emotional Journey of the Page

A visitor does not “browse.”  
They **descend into origin**, then **rise into daily life**.

### Act I — Arrival (Hero)

**Feel:** Soft shock of beauty. Silence.  
**Thought:** _This is not a store._

### Act II — Approach (The Forgotten Path)

**Feel:** Solitude, respect for distance.  
**Thought:** _It takes intention to reach this._

### Act III — Place (The Living Mountains)

**Feel:** Grounding. Oxygen. Geographic truth.  
**Thought:** _Pulag, Rumsu, Hallan — these are real._

### Act IV — Being (The Badri Cow)

**Feel:** Tenderness. Ethical gravity.  
**Thought:** _The animal is not an input. She is the beginning._

### Act V — Craft (The Bilona Method)

**Feel:** Concentration. Warmth of wood and time.  
**Thought:** _Hands made this. Slowly._

### Act VI — Kinship (The People)

**Feel:** Dignity. Human warmth held quietly.  
**Thought:** _I am indebted to keepers, not a factory._

### Act VII — Evidence (Product Passport)

**Feel:** Calm trust. Intellectual satisfaction.  
**Thought:** _I can name altitude, method, villages._

### Act VIII — Intimacy (Daily Ritual)

**Feel:** Domestic peace. Desire to use, not hoard.  
**Thought:** _This belongs in my morning._

### Act IX — Object (Packaging)

**Feel:** Refined desire. Artifact pleasure.  
**Thought:** _The jar is worthy of the story._

### Act X — Vow (Founder Note)

**Feel:** Belief. Soft accountability.  
**Thought:** _Someone is responsible for protecting this._

### Act XI — Horizon (Related Products)

**Feel:** Open curiosity without pressure.  
**Thought:** _There are other origins in this house._

### Emotional arc (one line)

**Awe → Distance → Place → Life → Craft → People → Trust → Ritual → Object → Belief → Continuity**

Never: hype → discount → urgency → cart anxiety.

# 4. Art Direction

## One Unified System (No Drift Allowed)

### Photography

- Documentary luxury stills, as if one DP shot the entire film
- Prefer natural light; controlled warmth only for clarification scenes
- Shallow to moderate depth of field — never fake miniature tilt
- Slight honest grain permitted; no plastic skin, no AI gloss
- Faces used sparingly; consent and dignity absolute
- Badri cows must read as indigenous hill cattle — never industrial dairy breeds

### Materials

- Glass, wood, linen, raw cotton, stone, brass, handmade paper
- No plastic props, no glossy acrylic stands, no marble-for-luxury cliché unless truly present on site

### Textures

- Wet stone, pasture grass, cow coat, bilona wood grain, ghee surface, linen weave
- Texture is emotion — show it patiently

### Typography feeling (for art direction of frames)

- Leave **quiet negative space** for Cormorant / Literata / Manrope overlays
- Never crowd the optical centre with competing subjects
- Sanskrit / shloka moments prefer soft gold light and empty air

### Colour system

| Role          | Direction                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Base          | Warm Himalayan sand                  |
| Nature        | Forest moss                          |
| Product       | Muted true ghee gold                 |
| Text worlds   | Soft charcoal                        |
| Atmosphere    | Morning mist blue-grey               |
| Accent (rare) | Muted gold line — never large fields |

### Lighting

- Dawn, open shade, soft overcast preferred
- Golden hour for humility-of-scale frames
- Fire glow only as a whisper in bilona clarification
- No beauty-dish ecommerce lighting

### Props

- Only what belongs to the method or the home ritual
- Maximum restraint: if it doesn’t earn its place, remove it

### Composition

- Apple/Hermès negative space
- Leading lines for path and valley
- One subject per frame
- Triptych thinking for passport/village specificity

### Negative space

- Treat empty air as a luxury material
- Especially in hero, packaging, founder letter

### Mood

- Timeless, emotional, authentic, museum-quality
- Kinfolk pace, NatGeo truth, Aman silence

### Consistency lock

If a frame could be used by a generic ghee brand on Amazon, **it fails**.  
If a frame could hang in a quiet gallery beside a village name, **it passes**.
