# Purpose

The Hero is the emotional gateway into The Only Retreats.

It is the most important visual component on the website.

Its responsibility is not to sell products.

Its responsibility is to establish trust, curiosity and emotional connection before any commerce appears.

Visitors should immediately understand:

"This is not a typical ecommerce website."

---

# Business Goal

Increase emotional engagement.

Increase average session duration.

Encourage scrolling.

Establish premium brand perception.

Prepare visitors for storytelling.

---

# User Goal

Visitors should immediately understand:

Where this brand comes from.

What makes it different.

Why they should continue exploring.

---

# Emotional Goal

The Hero should evoke:

Silence.

Wonder.

Fresh mountain air.

Morning sunlight.

Curiosity.

Trust.

Peace.

The visitor should feel invited into the Himalayas.

---

# Core Message

The Himalayas are not our backdrop.

They are our beginning.

Everything else follows.
