# Project Overview

# Vision

The Only Retreats is a premium storytelling-first Himalayan nourishment brand.

We are not building a traditional ecommerce website.

We are building a digital experience that reconnects people with the origins of their food.

The website should educate before it sells.

Every interaction should increase trust.

Every product should feel like the result of a journey—not a transaction.

---

# Mission

To preserve ancient Himalayan nourishment traditions by connecting conscious consumers directly with authentic communities, landscapes, and heritage.

The website should communicate:

• authenticity

• patience

• transparency

• craftsmanship

• simplicity

• heritage

• sustainability

# Design Principles

Every page must follow these principles.

## Story before Commerce

Visitors should emotionally connect before seeing products.

The website should feel like reading the opening chapter of a beautiful book.

---

## Nature is the Interface

Photography is not decoration.

Photography communicates origin.

Every image should strengthen authenticity.

---

## Calm Interactions

Motion should feel like:

wind

mist

sunrise

breathing

No aggressive movement.

No distracting animations.

---

## White Space is Luxury

Do not fill empty space.

Allow content to breathe.

Spacing creates elegance.

---

## Editorial Layout

Avoid traditional ecommerce layouts.

Think of:

Kinfolk

Aesop

Apple Editorial

National Geographic

Luxury travel magazines

instead of Amazon.

---

# Content Architecture

Visible content must never be hardcoded.

Every user-facing string belongs inside

lib/content

Components should consume typed content.

Business logic should never contain presentation copy.
